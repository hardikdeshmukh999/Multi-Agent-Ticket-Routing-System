How it works

End-to-end in six steps

Every ticket follows the same deterministic path through six specialist agents — no shortcuts, no black boxes.

system flowchart — full routing pipeline
01 — Identify
Intake Specialist
Looks up the customer record — their plan, tier, region, and the last 5 tickets they raised. Context before everything.
02 — Classify
Issue Classifier
Reads the issue and routes to the correct sub-department with a confidence score. Nine specialist teams across four divisions.
03 — Prioritise
Priority Analyst
Scores urgency — low to urgent — factoring in customer tier, issue severity, and SLA obligations. Enterprise gets a tier bump.
04 — Assign
Dispatcher
Finds the agent in the right department with the most remaining capacity. Saves the ticket to the database with full metadata.
05 — Respond
Communications Agent
Drafts a personalised opening reply — names the assigned agent, states the SLA, adds urgency language when needed.
06 — Review
Escalation Reviewer
Final check. Flags for immediate senior review if confidence is low, priority is urgent, or a security breach is detected.
